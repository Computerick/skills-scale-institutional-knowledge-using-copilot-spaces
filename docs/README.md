# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains the core project management guidance used by OctoAcme to move from idea to delivery and continuous improvement.

## Overview

OctoAcme manages work through a structured lifecycle that emphasizes customer value, clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and iterative learning. The process begins with project initiation, moves into planning and backlog alignment, continues through execution and tracking, and culminates in release, closeout, and retrospective improvement.

Across the workflow, the team focuses on:
- Defining business needs and measurable success criteria
- Aligning stakeholders and roles early in the project
- Breaking work into prioritized, testable increments
- Tracking progress, risks, and dependencies transparently
- Releasing with guardrails, validation, and rollback planning
- Capturing learnings and improving the process over time

## Lifecycle Summary

1. Initiation: define the problem, stakeholders, success metrics, and readiness for planning.
2. Planning: prioritize scope, estimate effort, set milestones, and document dependencies.
3. Execution: deliver in increments, hold team routines, track progress, and manage blockers.
4. Release: validate quality, deploy, verify outcomes, and communicate changes.
5. Close & Improve: review results, capture lessons learned, and update the process.
